Our story

From the family behind Mountain View Pub.

snug
/snʌg/ noun, Irish

A small, cozy room tucked inside a pub, where the best conversations and the worst-kept secrets have always lived.

A second home in Cave Creek

We've been pouring pints and plating fish and chips in Cave Creek for years at Mountain View Pub. Shenanigan's Snug is our second home in town: the same family recipes and the same warm welcome.

Come for the Guinness-braised Reuben. Stay because somebody started a singalong.

Made in house, poured properly

The cod is battered in Guinness. The corned beef is cooked in it. The Bloody Mary mix has thirteen ingredients and we're not telling you a single one.
